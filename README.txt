MSS Club Management Suite Demo v0.51 — Teams Master Directory

NEW

- Team lifecycle: Planning/Forming -> Active -> Completed -> Archived.
- Default view focuses on current teams; archived/completed teams remain available.

- Team summary metrics for total, forming, active, and archived/completed.
- Open Team detail shows coach, roster sizing, season cost, offers/commitments, and player roster.
- Team lifecycle status can be updated without deleting historical team records.
- Team Setup remains the creation/configuration workflow; Teams becomes the long-term team record.
