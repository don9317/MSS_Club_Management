MSS Club Management Suite v0.78 — In-App Multi-Team Budgets

- Team Accounting now contains the working Monthly Team Budget.
- Every actual team has its own separate monthly Budget/Actual data.
- Switch the Team selector to work on a different team's budget.
- Categories: Court Rental, Coaching, Administration, Tournaments / Leagues, Travel, Uniforms, Insurance, Equipment, Other.
- Each category has Jan-Dec Budget and Actual entries plus annual totals and variance.
- Annual Team Budget is calculated from the monthly budget and feeds the per-player cost calculation automatically.
- Approved Season Cost remains team-specific and flows to Payment Plans and Offer Readiness.
- Export Club Budget Workbook creates an Excel-compatible workbook with a Club Summary worksheet and a worksheet for each actual team.
- The old standalone monthly workbook is retained in the ZIP only as a legacy/reference backup; it is no longer the primary budgeting workflow.
- Existing v0.77 browser data migrates into v0.78.
